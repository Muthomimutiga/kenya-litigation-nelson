# Kenyan Court Hearing Types

## High Court (Civil Division, Commercial Division, Constitutional & Human Rights Division)

| Hearing Type | Description | Typical Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **Mention** | Short administrative hearing to check progress | Directions, next hearing date |
| **Directions** | Court gives procedural directions | Pleadings/filing timetable fixed |
| **Interlocutory Application** | Application for interim relief (injunctions, stay orders, etc.) | Order granted/dismissed; costs |
| **Pre-trial Conference (PTC)** | Case management before full trial | Trial date fixed, issues narrowed |
| **Full Hearing / Trial** | Substantive hearing of evidence | Part-heard adjournment or reserved judgment |
| **Ruling** | Court decides an interlocutory matter | Order with reasons |
| **Judgment** | Final decision on the suit | Judgment for plaintiff/defendant; costs |
| **Taxation** | Bill of costs assessed by Registrar | Certificate of costs |
| **Ex Parte** | One-sided urgent application | Temporary order; return date fixed |

## Court of Appeal

| Hearing Type | Description |
|---|---|
| **Plenary** | Full bench hearing of an appeal |
| **Single Judge** | Interlocutory applications before a single judge |
| **Mention** | Case management |
| **Judgment** | Delivery of appellate judgment |

## Environment and Land Court (ELC)

Same typology as High Court. Note: ELC hears matters under the Land Registration Act, Environmental Management and Co-ordination Act, and related legislation.

## Employment and Labour Relations Court (ELRC)

| Hearing Type | Description |
|---|---|
| **Case Management Conference (CMC)** | Equivalent of PTC |
| **Mention** | Progress check |
| **Mediation** | Court-directed mediation |
| **Full Hearing** | Evidence hearing |
| **Ruling / Judgment** | Decision |

## Magistrate Courts (Chief Magistrate, Senior Resident Magistrate, Resident Magistrate)

Same typology as High Court at a lower level. Magistrate courts handle civil suits up to Kshs 20 million and most criminal matters.

## Small Claims Court

Informal hearings; strict timelines apply under the Small Claims Court Act.

---

## Significance by Hearing Type for Post-Hearing Workflow

**Mention / Directions** → Usually generates filing timetables. Extract each date and document type carefully.

**Interlocutory Application** → May generate an urgent service requirement if an ex parte order was granted. Check if a return date was fixed and whether the other side needs to be served with the order.

**Ruling** → Extract costs orders carefully. Note whether costs are in the cause, to a party, or reserved.

**Judgment** → Extract: (a) who won, (b) what was awarded, (c) costs, (d) any time limit for appeal (usually 30 days for High Court, 30 days for Court of Appeal). Flag appeal deadline immediately as a high-priority deadline.

**Ex Parte Orders** → Urgent: identify return date, identify parties to be served, serve immediately.
