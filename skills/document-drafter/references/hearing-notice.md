# Hearing Notice — Template

## Output Category

**Category B — Court Document.** Begin output with:
`[COURT DOCUMENT — No letterhead. File at registry. Check case number before filing.]`

---

## When to Use

A Hearing Notice is issued by a party (through their advocate) or by the court registry to notify all parties of the date, time, and venue of a scheduled hearing. In Kenyan practice:

- **Court-issued:** The registry sends a hearing notice when the court fixes a date. Advocates receive this via email or registry notification on the e-filing system.
- **Advocate-issued:** An advocate may issue and serve a Hearing Notice when:
  - The matter is fixed for hearing after an inter-partes application and the opposing party needs formal notice
  - The court has given a date ex-parte and the rule requires service of the notice on the other party before the date
  - The matter is restored from an abeyance or adjournment and the advocate is driving the next date

A Hearing Notice is simpler than a Notice of Motion — it does not set out grounds or prayers. It simply notifies the receiving party of when the matter will be heard.

## Template — Advocate-Issued Hearing Notice

```
[COURT CAPTION]

HEARING NOTICE

TO: [NAME OF ADVOCATE / FIRM ON RECORD FOR THE OPPOSING PARTY]
    [ADDRESS]

AND TO: [ANY OTHER PARTY OR ADVOCATE TO BE NOTIFIED]

TAKE NOTICE that the above-captioned [suit / application / petition / appeal] has been fixed for hearing before the Honourable [Judge / Magistrate / Registrar] on:

Date:  [DAY], [DATE] [MONTH] [YEAR]
Time:  [TIME, e.g., 9.00 a.m.]
Venue: [COURT NAME, e.g., High Court of Kenya at Nairobi — Court [NUMBER] / Milimani Law Courts]

[OPTIONAL — include only if relevant:]
The hearing is in respect of the [Applicant's / Plaintiff's] [Notice of Motion / Chamber Summons / Application] dated [DATE].

You are required to appear at the above date, time, and place with your client and all relevant documents. Failure to appear may result in the matter being heard in your absence and such orders made as the court may deem just.

Dated at [TOWN] this [DAY] day of [MONTH], [YEAR].

_______________________
NELSON M. MANDELA
MABEYA & MANDELA ADVOCATES LLP
ADVOCATES FOR THE [APPLICANT / PLAINTIFF / PETITIONER / APPELLANT]
```

## Template — Restoration Notice (After Abeyance / Long Adjournment)

Use when a matter was dormant and you are bringing it back:

```
[COURT CAPTION]

NOTICE OF RESTORATION TO HEARING LIST

TO: [OPPOSING COUNSEL FIRM AND ADDRESS]

TAKE NOTICE that the above-captioned matter, which has been in abeyance since [DATE OF LAST APPEARANCE / ADJOURNMENT], has been restored to the hearing list and is now fixed for mention / hearing on:

Date:  [DAY], [DATE] [MONTH] [YEAR]
Time:  [TIME]
Venue: [COURT NAME AND ROOM]

Kindly arrange for your attendance and that of your client on the said date.

Dated at [TOWN] this [DAY] day of [MONTH], [YEAR].

_______________________
NELSON M. MANDELA
MABEYA & MANDELA ADVOCATES LLP
ADVOCATES FOR THE [PARTY]
```

## Drafting Notes

- **Service:** Serve the Hearing Notice on all parties on record — file an Affidavit of Service to prove it. Without proof of service, the court may decline to proceed ex-parte.
- **Timing:** Serve with sufficient lead time — typically at least 7 days before the hearing date, or as directed by the court or relevant rules.
- **eCourt / e-filing system:** If the matter is on Kenya's eCourt system, the hearing notice may be generated automatically by the registry and sent to registered advocates by email. Check the system before issuing your own.
- **No grounds or prayers:** A Hearing Notice does not argue or pray for anything. Keep it short. If you need to make an application, use a Notice of Motion or Chamber Summons.
- **Client notification:** Also send the client a copy and confirm they will attend if their presence is required (e.g., for cross-examination, mentions where instructions may need to be given on the spot).
