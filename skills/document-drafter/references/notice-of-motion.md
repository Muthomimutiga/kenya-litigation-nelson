# Notice of Motion — Template

## Output Category

**Category B — Court Document.** Begin output with:
`[COURT DOCUMENT — No letterhead. File at registry. Check case number before filing.]`

---

## When to Use

A Notice of Motion is the standard way to bring an interlocutory application before the High Court, ELC, or ELRC. It is supported by a Supporting Affidavit and (for urgent matters) a Certificate of Urgency.

## Template

```
[COURT CAPTION]

NOTICE OF MOTION
(Under [cite the rule/provision authorising the application, e.g., Order 40 Rule 1 of the Civil Procedure Rules, 2010 / Section [X] of the [Act] / Articles [X] and [X] of the Constitution of Kenya, 2010])

TAKE NOTICE that [APPLICANT'S FULL NAME], the Applicant herein, will apply to this Honourable Court [on [DATE] at [TIME] / at a time to be fixed by this Honourable Court] for orders that:—

1. [PRAYER 1 — usually the substantive relief, e.g., "An order of injunction restraining the Respondent, its agents, servants, employees, and all persons claiming under it from..."]

2. [PRAYER 2 — e.g., "A declaration that..."]

3. [PRAYER 3 — e.g., "An order directing the Respondent to..."]

4. Costs of this application be provided for.

5. Such further and other relief as this Honourable Court may deem fit.

(c) [Ground 3]
```

## Certificate of Urgency (for urgent ex parte or urgent inter partes applications)

Append this certificate immediately after the Notice:

```
CERTIFICATE OF URGENCY

I, [ADVOCATE NAME], the Advocate for the Applicant herein, hereby certify that this matter is urgent for the following reasons:—

[State specific reasons — not mere assertions. E.g., "The Respondent has threatened to demolish the Applicant's structure before the next regular motion day, causing irreparable harm."]

I further certify that the granting of the orders sought ex parte / on short notice is necessary to avert the risk of [irreparable harm / imminent harm / prejudice].

_______________________
[ADVOCATE NAME]
[DATE]
```

## Common Grounding Statutes

| Relief Sought | Statute to Cite |
|---|---|
| Injunction (High Court) | Order 40 Rule 1, Civil Procedure Rules, 2010 |
| Judicial Review | Order 53, Civil Procedure Rules, 2010; Fair Administrative Action Act, 2015 |
| Constitutional enforcement | Articles 22 & 23, Constitution of Kenya, 2010; Mutunga Rules, 2013 |
| Stay of execution | Order 42 Rule 6, Civil Procedure Rules, 2010 |
| Land injunction (ELC) | Section 13, Environment and Land Court Act, 2011; Order 40 CPR |
| Employment injunction (ELRC) | Rule 17, ELRC (Procedure) Rules, 2016 |
| Attachment before judgment | Order 39, Civil Procedure Rules, 2010 |
