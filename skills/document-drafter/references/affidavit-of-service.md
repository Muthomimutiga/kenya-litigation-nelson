# Affidavit of Service — Template

## Output Category

**Category B — Court Document.** Begin output with:
`[COURT DOCUMENT — No letterhead. File at registry. Check case number before filing.]`

---

## When to Use

Draft an Affidavit of Service after a document has been served on the opposing party or their advocate. This document proves to the court that service was effected.

## Template

```
[COURT CAPTION — match the case caption]

AFFIDAVIT OF SERVICE

I, [FULL NAME OF SERVER], of [address], do hereby make oath and state as follows:—

1. I am [an adult of sound mind] [an advocate of the High Court of Kenya / a clerk employed by [Firm Name]] and I am duly authorised to swear this affidavit.

2. On [DATE] at [TIME], I served [FULL NAME OF PARTY/ADVOCATE SERVED] of [address/firm name] with the following documents in the above-mentioned matter:—

   (a) [Document 1, e.g., Notice of Motion dated [date]]
   (b) [Document 2, e.g., Supporting Affidavit of [name] dated [date]]
   (c) [Any other documents]

   [FOR EMAIL SERVICE — use paragraph 3A below]
   [FOR PHYSICAL SERVICE — use paragraph 3B below]

3A. [EMAIL SERVICE]
Service was effected by way of electronic mail (email) addressed to [email address] at [time] on [date]. Annexed hereto and marked "Exhibit A" is a printout of the sent email confirming transmission of the said documents.

3B. [PHYSICAL SERVICE]
Service was effected by [delivering a copy of the said documents to the said [name/description of person served] personally / leaving a copy of the said documents at the offices of [firm/address] with [name and description of recipient]] at [address] at approximately [time] on [date]. [The said [name/description] acknowledged receipt of the documents / A copy of the documents was left with [name] who identified themselves as [position].

4. I make this affidavit in support of an application to certify service in the above-mentioned matter.

SWORN at [TOWN] this [DAY] day of [MONTH], [YEAR]

By the said [NAME]

_______________________
DEPONENT

Before me:

_______________________
COMMISSIONER FOR OATHS/
NOTARY PUBLIC
```

## Email Service Exhibit Format

When serving by email, the Exhibit A printout should show:
- From: [Firm email]
- To: [Opposing advocate's email]
- Date and time sent
- Subject line (usually "RE: [Case Name] — Service of [Document Name]")
- The email body (which should state what is attached)

Save a PDF of the sent email for attachment. Many firms BCC a firm mailbox for record-keeping.

## Multiple Parties

If serving multiple parties, create a separate paragraph for each party served, numbered sequentially (3, 4, 5...). Or draft separate affidavits of service for each.

## Filing the Affidavit of Service

File the Affidavit of Service with the court registry before or at the next hearing to prove service. Some courts require it to be filed before the court date; others accept it on the date. Check the specific court's practice.

For eFiling: upload as a separate document tagged "Affidavit of Service".
