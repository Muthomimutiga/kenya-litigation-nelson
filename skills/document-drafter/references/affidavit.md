# Supporting Affidavit — Template

## Output Category

**Category B — Court Document.** Begin output with:
`[COURT DOCUMENT — No letterhead. File at registry. Check case number before filing.]`

---

## When to Use

A Supporting Affidavit accompanies most interlocutory applications and sets out the facts on which the application is based. It is sworn by the applicant or a person with knowledge of the facts.

## Template

```
[COURT CAPTION]

SUPPORTING AFFIDAVIT OF [DEPONENT FULL NAME]

I, [FULL NAME], of [address / occupation], do hereby make oath and state as follows:—

1. I am the [Plaintiff/Petitioner/Applicant / Director of the Applicant Company / duly authorised agent of the Applicant] in the above-mentioned matter and I am duly authorised to swear this affidavit. I have personal knowledge of the matters herein deposed to, save where stated to be on information and belief, and where so stated I verily believe the same to be true.

[FACTUAL PARAGRAPHS — one fact per paragraph, short and clear]

2. [Background — who the parties are, the relationship, the transaction/dispute]

3. [What happened — chronological narrative of key facts]

4. [The specific acts or omissions complained of]

5. [The harm suffered or threatened]

6. [Why the relief sought is necessary]

7. Annexed hereto and collectively marked "Exhibit [NAME]-1" is [description of exhibit, e.g., "a copy of the agreement dated [date]"].

8. Annexed hereto and collectively marked "Exhibit [NAME]-2" is [description].

[Continue numbering exhibits sequentially]

[Final paragraph — standard closing]

X. In the premises, I humbly pray that this Honourable Court be pleased to grant the orders sought in the Notice of Motion filed herewith.

SWORN at [TOWN] this [DAY] day of [MONTH], [YEAR]

By the said [FULL NAME]

_______________________
DEPONENT

Before me:

_______________________
COMMISSIONER FOR OATHS / NOTARY PUBLIC
```

## Exhibit Marking Convention

Mark each exhibit with the deponent's initials and a number: e.g., if the deponent is Jane Wanjiku, exhibits are JW-1, JW-2, JW-3, etc. This avoids confusion when there are multiple affidavits.

Physically, each exhibit should bear a sticker or stamp: "This is Exhibit [JW-1] referred to in the Supporting Affidavit of [JANE WANJIKU] sworn on [DATE]."

## Rules for Good Affidavits

- Each paragraph deals with one fact only.
- No argument in an affidavit — argument belongs in written submissions.
- No legal conclusions — state the facts; let counsel make the legal argument.
- No hearsay unless necessary — and if used, state the source and that you believe it to be true.
- Keep exhibits to what is strictly necessary.
- The affidavit speaks as of the date it is sworn.

## Replying Affidavit

A Replying Affidavit responds to the Supporting Affidavit. Open as follows:

```
I, [FULL NAME], of [address], do hereby make oath and state as follows:—

1. I am the Respondent/Defendant herein and I am duly authorised to swear this affidavit. I have personal knowledge of the matters herein deposed to.

2. I have read and understood the Supporting Affidavit of [name] sworn on [date] and I wish to reply thereto as follows:—

3. [Admissions — identify what is admitted]
Paragraphs [X, Y, Z] of the Supporting Affidavit are admitted.

4. [Denials — identify what is denied and give the true version]
Paragraph [X] of the Supporting Affidavit is denied. The true position is that...

5. [New facts — facts not mentioned in the Supporting Affidavit that are relevant to the defence]
```
