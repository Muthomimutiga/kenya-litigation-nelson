# Demand Letter — Template

## When to Use

A demand letter is sent before filing suit. It puts the other party on formal notice of the claim, states the basis, quantifies the demand, sets a deadline, and signals that litigation follows if the demand is not met. It is:
- Required before certain suits (e.g., suits against the Government or State organs under the Government Proceedings Act — 30-day notice mandatory)
- Standard practice in debt recovery, breach of contract, professional negligence, and land disputes
- A prerequisite for mediation clauses in many commercial contracts
- Useful as an exhibit — attach it to the Plaint or Supporting Affidavit as evidence that demand was made and ignored

## Statutory Notice (Government / State Organs)

Under the Government Proceedings Act (Cap. 40), no suit may be filed against the Government unless at least **30 days** written notice has been given to the Attorney General stating the nature of the claim and the relief sought. Always check if the respondent is a State organ.

## Template — Standard Demand Letter

```
[DATE]

[RECIPIENT FULL NAME / DESIGNATION]
[COMPANY / ORGANISATION NAME]
[PHYSICAL ADDRESS]
[P.O. BOX]
[TOWN]

Dear Sir / Madam,

RE: DEMAND FOR PAYMENT — [CLIENT FULL NAME] v [DEBTOR / RESPONDENT NAME]
    [BRIEF SUBJECT MATTER, e.g., "BREACH OF CONTRACT DATED [DATE]" / "OUTSTANDING RENT — PREMISES AT [ADDRESS]"]

We act for and on behalf of [CLIENT FULL NAME] ("Our Client") in this matter. We write on our Client's instructions.

BACKGROUND

[Paragraph 1 — state the relationship between the parties and the transaction/obligation giving rise to the claim. E.g., "On [DATE], our Client entered into an Agreement with you for the [sale/lease/construction/provision of services] of [subject matter], a copy of which is annexed hereto as Annexure 'A'."]

[Paragraph 2 — state the breach or failure. E.g., "Pursuant to Clause [X] of the Agreement, you were obligated to [pay / deliver / complete / vacate] by [DATE]. Despite demand, you have failed, neglected, and/or refused to honour this obligation."]

THE DEMAND

In the premises, our Client demands that you, within **[14 / 30] days** from the date of this letter:

1. [DEMAND 1 — e.g., "Pay to our Client the sum of Kenya Shillings [AMOUNT IN WORDS] (Kshs. [AMOUNT IN FIGURES]), being [describe — outstanding balance / principal debt / rent arrears / damages]."]

2. [DEMAND 2 if applicable — e.g., "Vacate and deliver up vacant possession of the premises at [ADDRESS]."]

3. [DEMAND 3 if applicable — e.g., "Deliver to our Client all documents relating to [X]."]

INTEREST

Please note that our Client reserves the right to claim interest on the said sum at the rate of [14% / court rate / contractual rate] per annum from [DATE OF DEFAULT] until the date of payment in full.

CONSEQUENCES OF NON-COMPLIANCE

TAKE NOTICE that if you fail to comply with this demand within the stipulated period, our Client will, without further reference to you, institute legal proceedings against you for the recovery of the said sum together with interest and costs of the suit, and this letter shall be produced in those proceedings.

This letter is written without prejudice to any and all of our Client's rights and remedies, all of which are expressly reserved.

Yours faithfully,

_______________________
NELSON M. MANDELA
MABEYA & MANDELA ADVOCATES LLP
ADVOCATES FOR [CLIENT NAME]

Cc: [CLIENT NAME]
```

## Template — Government / State Organ Notice (Cap. 40)

```
[DATE]

The Honourable Attorney General
Attorney General's Chambers
[ADDRESS]
NAIROBI

AND TO:

[NAME OF GOVERNMENT MINISTRY / STATE ORGAN]
[ADDRESS]

Dear Sir,

RE: NOTICE OF INTENTION TO SUE — [CLIENT FULL NAME]
    PURSUANT TO SECTION 13A OF THE GOVERNMENT PROCEEDINGS ACT (CAP. 40)

We act for and on behalf of [CLIENT FULL NAME] ("Our Client") in this matter.

TAKE NOTICE that our Client intends, after the expiry of thirty (30) days from the date of service of this Notice, to institute legal proceedings against [NAME OF MINISTRY / STATE ORGAN / GOVERNMENT] arising from the following:

NATURE OF THE CLAIM

[Set out the facts giving rise to the claim — who, what, when, where, what happened, what loss resulted.]

RELIEF SOUGHT

Our Client shall seek the following relief:

1. [Prayer 1 — e.g., "A declaration that the actions of [Ministry / Officer] in [doing X] were unlawful."]
2. [Prayer 2 — e.g., "Special damages of Kshs. [AMOUNT]."]
3. [Prayer 3 — e.g., "General damages."]
4. Costs of the suit.

This Notice is given pursuant to Section 13A of the Government Proceedings Act (Cap. 40).

Yours faithfully,

_______________________
NELSON M. MANDELA
MABEYA & MANDELA ADVOCATES LLP
ADVOCATES FOR [CLIENT NAME]
```

## Drafting Notes

- **Tone:** Firm but professional. Avoid threatening or abusive language — it weakens the letter and reflects poorly on counsel.
- **Quantify the demand exactly.** A vague demand ("pay what you owe") is easier to dispute and harder to rely on in court.
- **Annexures:** Attach the contract, invoice, or instrument giving rise to the claim. Number and label each annexure.
- **Deadline:** 14 days is standard for private parties. 30 days is mandatory for Government. Use the client's instruction on urgency to calibrate.
- **Keep a copy with proof of delivery.** Send by registered post + email. If hand-delivered, have the recipient sign a copy. This becomes your exhibit.
- **"Without prejudice":** Only use this heading if the letter is a genuine settlement offer. A demand letter making a unilateral demand is not without prejudice — using the label incorrectly can create confusion about admissibility.
